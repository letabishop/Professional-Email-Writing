The 5 Core Components of a Professional Email

Click on any section of the sample email below to learn its specific rules, best practices, and common pitfalls.

To: mrs.gomez@lincolnhigh.edu
From: maya.lin@student.lincolnhigh.edu
Subject Line
AP History Essay Extension Request - Maya Lin (Period 3)
Salutation / Greeting
Dear Mrs. Gomez,
Opening Context & Body
I hope you are having a good week.

I am writing regarding the AP U.S. History research paper due this Friday. Due to missing class yesterday for the track meet, I am requesting a one-day extension to review my primary sources with you during lunch tutoring.
Call to Action (CTA) / Next Steps
Could I stop by your classroom tomorrow at 11:45 AM during lunch to review my outline and confirm if Monday submission works?
Sign-off & Student Signature
Thank you,
Maya Lin
11th Grade Student | AP History (Period 3)
maya.lin@student.lincolnhigh.edu
